\begin{resumo}[Abstract]			% Título do Resumo (Abstract = Resumo em inglês)
\begin{otherlanguage*}{english}		% Língua do texto
This Undergraduate Thesis presents the design and implementation of an Administrative Web System developed for the Bom Gosto bakery, aiming to replace manual control methods with a centralized digital management solution. The research adopts an applied and descriptive methodology, utilizing system modeling with the Unified Modeling Language (UML) to map business workflows, as well as modular development split between administrative order management and production management. As results, the system enables the detailed recording and tracking of custom orders and ready-to-deliver items, centralizes pickup scheduling, automates raw material depletion in inventory, and accurately calculates the manufacturing cost of each recipe. It is concluded that the proposed automation eliminates reliance on informal notes, reduces the risks of delays and ingredient waste, improves the organization of daily routines, and provides greater security and 
operational efficiency to the establishment's management. 

\vspace{\onelineskip}
\noindent
\textbf{Keywords}: Commercial automation. Administrative management. Bakery. Web system. Operational efficiency.
\end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
